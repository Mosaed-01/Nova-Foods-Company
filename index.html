About Us

Who We Are

Premium Quality Imports

Bridging Global Excellence to Local Markets

Nova Foods is a dynamic international food trading and distribution company headquartered in Yemen. The company specializes in the import, export, and wholesale distribution of high-quality food products sourced from trusted global manufacturers.

With a strong supplier network across Asia and the Middle East, Nova Foods connects international producers with local and regional markets, ensuring consistent quality, competitive pricing, and reliable supply.
